## Product and business operations

I am a University of Virginia McIntire graduate interested in making complicated work easier to understand and act on. My experience includes product planning at SupplyBistro, AI research and evaluation during Booz Allen Hamilton's Summer Games program, and finance leadership in UVA's Black Economic Empowerment Society.

I enjoy working through an unclear request, defining what a useful outcome would look like, and building a practical way to get there. I am particularly interested in work that improves how people and small businesses navigate complex systems.
